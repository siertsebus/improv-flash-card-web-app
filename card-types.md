
## Overall note

All cards outline info about what one would normally *expect* from a particular character, genre etc. Yes, it is fun to do a scene about a wedding in a rainforest or about a first date where everyone has a gun but those are fun exactly because they go *against expectations*. These flash cards are for grasping what is expected.

## Card types

Character Archetype (e.g. doctor, Batman, laywer, highschool jockey, climate activist)
- What do they care about?
- What mannerisms/movements/posture do they have?
- What does their voice sound like? (-> reference voice card)
- What phrases or lines do they use?
- What recognizable items do they have? (i.e. what objects immediately signal this particular archetype. Example: scalpel or stethoscope for a doctor)

Character Role (e.g. career-obsessed, longing-for-adventure, irredeemable villain, awaiting-love, must-redeem-myself, stern mentor)
- What do they care about?
- How do they start out?
- What will happen to them?
- How does their personality change?

Situation (e.g. wedding, car race, first date)
- What typically happens?
- Where do these situations occur? (-> reference location card)
- What types of people are present? (-> reference character archetype)

Genre (e.g. dystopian philosophical sci-fi, steampunk, spaghetti western, zombie apocalypse)
- What character archetypes and character roles do we expect?
- What situations do we expect? (-> reference situation card)
- What locations do we expect?  (-> reference location card)

Pop culture / General (e.g. the Harry Potter series, Alien conspiracy theories, Buzzfeed, Gaming culture, Right-wing politics)
- What do we expect?
